Your account

  • Type a username and a password, then tap Make account. Next time, tap Log in with the same username and password.
  • Or tap Sign in with Google, then pick a username.
  • Nobody else can take your username. To change account, tap Sign out at the top.

Download the hub

  • Tap Download at the top. On iPhone use Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
  • After that every app opens from the hub, even with no internet.

Go back to the hub

  • Use your phone's back button, or tap the small “Hub” tab at the top of the screen.

Servers in Nautic Online

  • Open Nautic Online and tap Servers. Public servers show in the list for everyone. Link only servers need the link. Private servers need the link and the password.
  • When everyone leaves, the server pauses. It carries on from the same spot when someone joins.
